Each entry includes its part of speech, meaning, word-formation process, and a natural example sentence.

01 / DIGITAL HABITSCompounding

doomscrolling

/ˈduːmˌskroʊlɪŋ/
Noun

The habit of spending a long time reading negative news online.

Word formation
doom + scroll + -ing → doomscrolling
Doom and scroll combine to form the verb doomscroll; -ing forms the activity noun.
“I started doomscrolling after studying and lost an hour before bed.”
02 / SOCIAL MEDIABlending + clipping

finsta

/ˈfɪn.stə/
Noun · Informal

A secondary, often private Instagram account shared with a smaller group.

Word formation
fake + Instagram → Finstagram → finsta
Finstagram blends fake and Instagram; finsta is a clipped form.
“She shares casual photos with close friends on her finsta.”
03 / LEARNINGBlending

edutainment

/ˌedʒuˈteɪnmənt/
Noun

Content or activities designed to educate and entertain at the same time.

Word formation
education + entertainment → edutainment
A blend combining the beginning of education with the ending of entertainment.
“Our science game uses edutainment to make revision more enjoyable.”

About the project

Words tell us how we live

A small student dictionary exploring word formation through contemporary language.

Our approach

This mini dictionary looks at three words connected to digital routines, social media, and learning. It shows how speakers combine familiar word parts to name new experiences.

  • Compounding and suffixation: doom + scroll + -ing
  • Blending and clipping: fake + Instagram → Finstagram → finsta
  • Blending: education + entertainment → edutainment

Some entries are informal or playful. The examples on this site are original sentences written for student use.
