The mini dictionary

Words from campus life

Three familiar experiences, each captured in a compact word. Read the pronunciation, meaning, word formation, and example.

01 / FOOD & FRIENDSBlending

brunch

/brʌntʃ/ · noun

早午餐；早餐与午餐合并的一餐

Word formation
breakfast + lunch → brunch
A blend formed by combining the beginning of breakfast with the end of lunch.
“After our morning lecture, we had brunch at the campus café.”
02 / CAMPUS CULTURESuffixation

selfie

/ˈselfi/ · noun

自拍照；通常用手机拍摄并分享的自己的照片

Word formation
self + -ie → selfie
Formed by adding the informal noun-forming suffix -ie to self.
“Our study group took a selfie after finishing the project.”
03 / STUDY HABITSBlending · coined

studycrastination

/ˌstʌdiˌkræstɪˈneɪʃən/ · noun

学习拖延；用不紧急的学习任务来拖延更重要任务的行为

Word formation
study + procrastination → studycrastination
A student-created blend that combines study with procrastination.
“Tidying my notes instead of writing the essay was pure studycrastination.”
About this project

Language is always in progress.

The New Word Lab — Campus Word Dictionary is a student-made introduction to word formation in everyday campus English. It pairs an established word with a playful, clearly labelled student coinage to show how people shape language around real experiences.

01
BlendingParts of two words combine to create one new word: brunch.
02
SuffixationA suffix is added to a base word: self + -ie.
03
Student coinageStudycrastination is presented as a creative campus blend, not a standard dictionary headword.
